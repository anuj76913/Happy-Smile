# Leads console: one-page guide for front-desk staff

For: front-desk staff at the pilot clinics. Used in the 30-minute training session (plan P4-09).

## What changes

A virtual assistant calls some people within a couple of minutes of their enquiry. It always says it is a virtual assistant. It can book a **consultation**, or pass the person to you. Other enquiries are randomly left with you, as today. That's the **Standard callback** tab, and it lets us measure whether the assistant actually helps.

## Your tabs

| Tab | What's in it | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Needs you** | People the assistant handed over, and WhatsApp replies | Call them. **Red / Urgent first.** The timer shows the target: 5 min urgent, 15 min other |
| **Booked by assistant** | Consultations the assistant booked | Open each one, read the summary and transcript, then **Review**: Approve, Change time or Cancel |
| **No answer** | People the assistant couldn't reach | Nothing, unless they reply on WhatsApp. Replies move to Needs you |
| **Standard callback** | Today's process | Call as usual and **always record what happened** |

## On every call you make

1. Click the lead, then **Call** (this tells colleagues you've got it).
2. Read the **AI call summary** first. Don't make the patient repeat themselves.
3. Afterwards, record the outcome: **Book consultation**, **Follow up later** (add a note), **No answer**, **Not interested** or **Wrong number**.
4. For handovers, answer **"Was this handover right?"**. A "No" with a reason helps us fix the assistant every week.

## Urgent handovers

Pain, swelling or bleeding shows as **Urgent** with a 5-minute target. The patient was told someone would call within minutes, so drop what you can and call. After 10 minutes unclaimed, the clinic manager is alerted, and the assistant is switched off for your clinic until someone reviews what happened.

## What the assistant will never do

Diagnose, give medical advice, quote prices beyond the approved card, negotiate EMI or discounts, or pretend to be a person. It hands those conversations to you.

## WhatsApp

Booking confirmations and "we couldn't reach you" messages go out automatically for assistant bookings. If a confirmation **isn't delivered**, the lead appears in Needs you: please call to confirm. If someone replies **STOP**, they won't be contacted again.

## If something looks wrong

Tell the queue owner or the clinic manager. Managers can **Stop assistant** or **Pause AI booking** for a clinic from **Ops** in one click. New leads then come to you as before.
