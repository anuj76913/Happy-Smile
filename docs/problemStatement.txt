PRODUCT CASE: HappySmile — AI voice assistant for lead callbacks
(Product Manager case exercise, about 2–3 hours. Fictional scenario.
"We value judgement over polish.")

======================================================================
PART 1 — THE PROBLEM (as given)
======================================================================

Headline: Interested people ask HappySmile to call. The call comes hours later.

  Enquiry arrives  ---- 4–6 hours ---->  Front desk calls

The situation
- HappySmile is a chain of 12 dental clinics in India.
- Each day, about 300 people show interest online by enquiring about braces,
  whitening, implants and similar treatments.
- Front-desk staff call them back, usually 4–6 hours later.
- Only about 30% of enquiries become booked appointments.
- HappySmile wants an AI voice assistant that reaches each interested person
  within minutes, qualifies them, and either books an appointment or hands
  over to staff.

What three people said
1. Operations head (sharing from their data):
   "Leads we happen to call within 5 minutes convert 3x better.
    So the fix is simple: call everyone faster."
2. Founder:
   "The AI should sound so human that nobody realises it's AI."
3. Front-desk lead:
   "Every lead should still go to my team. I don't trust a bot with patients."

======================================================================
PART 3 — BUILD SPEC FOR THE MOCKUP (suggested; edit to match your own take)
======================================================================

Goal: a small clickable web prototype with exactly TWO screens, deployed to
a public URL that opens without login (e.g. Vercel, Netlify, GitHub Pages,
or a public share link from your vibe-coding tool). Use only made-up names,
numbers and phone numbers. Do not use real HappySmile branding.

Screen 1 — "Request a call" (mobile, ~390px wide)
The moment a person asks to be contacted, on HappySmile's enquiry page.
- Fields: name, mobile number, treatment (braces/aligners, whitening,
  implants, other), nearest clinic.
- Contact preference: "Call me now" (default) / "Call me at a time I pick"
  / "WhatsApp me instead".
- Language: English / Hindi.
- Clear disclosure before submit, e.g.:
  "HappySmile's virtual assistant will call you in about 2 minutes from
   +91 XXXXX XXXXX. It can book a consultation or connect you to our team.
   Say 'talk to a person' at any time."
- Consent checkbox for the call and WhatsApp updates.
- Submit button: "Request my call".
- Optional: after submit, show a confirmation state with the number that
  will call and a countdown.

Screen 2 — "Leads" queue for the front desk (desktop, ~1280px wide)
What HappySmile sees afterwards.
- Top strip for today: enquiries received, reached within 5 minutes,
  consults booked by assistant, waiting for staff, no answer (retrying).
- Tabs or filters, in this order:
    Needs you (handed over by assistant, sorted by waiting time, with a
      timer against a target, e.g. 15 minutes)
    Booked by assistant
    No answer (retry scheduled / WhatsApp sent)
    Standard callback (pilot control group — staff call as usual)
- Handover reasons shown as tags: asked for a person, pain or swelling
  (urgent), price/EMI question, clinical question, couldn't understand.
- Click a lead to open a side panel:
    name, treatment, clinic, language, time of enquiry, time first reached
    3-line AI call summary
    answers captured (treatment, preferred slot, urgency)
    transcript snippet + play-recording button
    actions: Call now, Book slot, Mark not interested
    feedback: "Was this handover right?" thumbs up / thumbs down
- Booked leads show slot, dentist, clinic, and "confirmation sent on
  WhatsApp", with a Review link so staff can check every booking.

Tone: clean, calm, clinical-but-friendly. Real buttons/inputs, readable
contrast, no emoji, no lorem ipsum.
